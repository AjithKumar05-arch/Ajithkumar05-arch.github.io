Inclusive Terminology

        by Aryan, Aman, Subh, Vedavati, & Gouramma

In this reflection blog, I will talk about my thoughts on presentatation on the topic Inclusive Terminology presentated by the group members- Aryan, Aman, Subh, Vedavati, and Gauramma. This was the last presentation of the course under unit 5 ‘Considerations for the information scientist’. So let’s begin.

Creative Topic

I found the topic very creative. The topic demonstrate the importance of word choices in our day to day life and how that can lead to inclusivity and exclusivity of different types and groups of people. I think that things like this: subtle but impactful, should not be ignored and dealt with care. This Idea only inspired some big techs to be careful about their word choice in technologies. Like some evidences the group used in their presentation slides are: master –> main, blacklist –> blocklist, manpower –> workforce. These minor things seems unimportant but plays a great role in real life in favouring some type and group of people over others.
